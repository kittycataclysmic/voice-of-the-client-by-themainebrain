[ VOICE OF THE CLIENT ] — understand what they need before you ever pitch anything

A 28-day, beginner-friendly path from zero VA experience to a real first client — built for career shifters who don't know this world yet. Organized around the same five phases used to run any Lean Six Sigma improvement project: Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control.

VOICE OF THE CUSTOMER [ VOC ]

A Lean Six Sigma term for capturing what a customer actually needs, in their own words, before designing anything around it. Applied here: understand what a client is really struggling with before you ever write a pitch, build an offer, or send a message. Most beginner mistakes trace back to skipping this step entirely.

An honest note before you start: this path will not guarantee you a client. No honest guide can promise that. What it will do is give you the structure, the proof, and the positioning to show up professionally and improve your real odds — and to know what you're actually doing at each step, instead of guessing.
